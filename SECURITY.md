# 🛡️ ShellGuard©™ Security Policy

## 🦞 The Sovereign Oath
We believe your data is your power. ShellGuard©™ is designed to ensure you never have to trust a third party with your secrets.

## 🐚 Security Standards
- **No Plaintext:** hu- keys are never sent to the server. Only SHA-256 hashes are used for verification.
- **Session Isolation:** api- tokens are stored in `sessionStorage` and cleared when the tab is closed.
- **Agent Scoping:** Lobster Keys©™ (lb-) are granular and can be revoked instantly by the human owner.
- **Sovereign Bedrock:** Your data lives in a local SQLite file. You own the hardware, you own the data.
